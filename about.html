About Smart Gala Mandi

Smart Gala Mandi is a modern online agricultural marketplace that bridges the gap between farmers and buyers. Our goal is to empower local farmers through technology by offering them direct access to markets and fair prices for their produce.

We simplify trading, reduce the role of middlemen, and bring transparency to the agricultural system. Farmers can list their products easily while buyers get access to quality goods directly from trusted sources.

Our mission is to revolutionize the agriculture sector of Pakistan through digital innovation — making farming more efficient, profitable, and sustainable.
