About Us

Smart Gala Mandi is a modern digital marketplace that connects farmers, traders, and buyers through a reliable online platform. We aim to make agriculture trading simple, transparent, and profitable for everyone.

Our mission is to empower local farmers with digital tools and help buyers access fresh produce directly from trusted sources — saving time, cost, and effort.
